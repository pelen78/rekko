Chrome extension

Record a demo with one click

Click the Rekko icon on any website and press Start recording. Every click captures the page and marks what you clicked. No screen sharing, no bookmarks, and it keeps recording when you change pages.

Download extension Chrome and Edge · 15 KB

Installed. Open the page you want to show and click the Rekko icon in Chrome’s toolbar.

How to install
  1. 1
    Unzip rekko-extension.zip.
  2. 2
    In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode, top right.
  3. 3
    Click Load unpacked and choose the rekko-extension folder. Pin it from the puzzle icon so it stays visible.

    Sharing

    You are sharing a single tab. Screens that open in another tab won’t be captured.

    Switch to your app and use it. Rekko captures each new screen when it changes and settles, and marks the area that changed as a guess of where you clicked. Drag the box to adjust it. For exact clicks on web pages, use the extension.

    Bookmark clicks

    No extension? A bookmark records the clicks on a web page while you share the tab.
